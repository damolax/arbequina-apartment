# September launch checklist

## Content and approvals
- Confirm guest capacity, bedrooms, bathrooms, bed setup and all amenities.
- Obtain original high-resolution photographs.
- Obtain exact verified review wording and permission to display it.
- Confirm public email, WhatsApp number and social profile handles.
- Approve direct-booking terms, privacy policy and cookie policy.

## Wix
- Apply global color and text themes.
- Rebuild all public pages and footer legal pages.
- Complete desktop and mobile layouts.
- Install Wix Smart Chat and train/test it.
- Add Wix Forms and automations.
- Add cookie banner and run Accessibility Wizard.

## Smoobu
- Verify Airbnb mapping and unified calendar.
- Complete base prices, minimum stays, fees, guest limits and payment methods.
- Test the individual-property Booking Engine in Smoobu.
- Embed it on Wix and test desktop/mobile.
- Complete a dummy booking, confirm Airbnb blocks, then cancel and confirm reopening.

## Visibility and measurement
- Finish existing SEO scope and connect Search Console.
- Implement approved AEO answer blocks and structured data.
- Connect GA4 through Wix Marketing Integrations.
- Configure booking, chat, WhatsApp and enquiry conversion events.
- Create and brand Instagram and Facebook launch profiles.

## Launch
- Freeze content 5 business days before launch.
- Test every link, form, chat response, booking route and policy.
- Publish, verify analytics in Realtime, and monitor bookings/messages daily for the first week.
